Which Seattle library branches recovered after 2020?

Annual in-person visits at Seattle Public Library's 27 branches fell 71% between 2019 and 2020. By 2025, the Central Library was back to 88% of its 2019 count, while the Northgate branch exceeded its 2019 count by 6% after its 2023 expansion.
