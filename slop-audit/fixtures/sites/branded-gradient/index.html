Sunrise Fest 2027

Three days of folk, soul, and brass on the Harbor Lawn, June 11–13.

Lineup by day

  1. Friday: The Delta Lanterns, Marisol Quintero, Harbor Brass Collective
  2. Saturday: June Okafor, The Weathervanes, Little Saint James
  3. Sunday: Gospel choir of St. Brendan's, Ada & the Night Ferry

Getting there

Gates open at 2 pm each day. The Harbor Lawn is a ten-minute walk from Pier Street station. Day passes are $45; children under 12 attend free with an adult.

The orange-to-pink sunrise gradient on our wordmark is part of the festival's identity, used on every poster since 2019.
