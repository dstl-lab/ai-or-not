Source: San Diego County Coastal Vulnerability Assessment (2024), intermediate-high scenario.

What this means

In 2020, 4% of La Jolla Shores parcels and 12% of Mission Beach parcels sit in the flood zone. Imperial Beach is in between at 9%.

Method

We counted parcels whose centroid falls inside each scenario's flood polygon. Parcel boundaries are from the 2023 assessor roll.
