Groundwater levels in the Central Valley, 2000–2025

Monitoring wells across the San Joaquin basin show where the water table has dropped fastest. This story walks through four regions, one per section.

North: Stanislaus County

Median depth to water rose from 42 to 61 feet. Most of the decline happened during the 2012–2016 drought.

Central: Fresno County

Depth to water rose from 88 to 140 feet, and the land surface sank by up to 2.3 feet near Mendota.

South: Kern County

Recharge basins along the Kern River raised levels in wet years, but the 25-year trend is still downward.

What comes next

Sustainability plans under SGMA must stop chronic declines by 2040.
